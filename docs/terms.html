What this dashboard is

This dashboard is a free, public interest tool that visualises Great Britain's national electricity grid: generation mix by fuel type, wholesale electricity price, carbon intensity, system frequency, interconnector flows with mainland Europe and Ireland, and regional carbon intensity across all 14 GB Distribution Network Operator (DNO) regions. It is built entirely on data obtained from the Elexon Insights Solution (BMRS) API, the National Energy System Operator (NESO) Data Portal, and the Carbon Intensity API (a project by NESO and the University of Oxford Department of Computer Science). It is not an official government service, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of NESO, Elexon, Ofgem, or any other public body.

Data sources

This dashboard is built entirely from the following official, publicly available sources. We do not collect or generate any original grid telemetry ourselves:

  • Elexon Insights Solution (BMRS) — 5-minute metered generation by fuel type (FUELINST), wholesale electricity price (MID), system frequency, and net imbalance/balancing volume. This covers transmission-connected generation and every GB interconnector.
  • NESO Demand Data Update — half-hourly estimates of embedded (distribution-connected) wind and solar generation, which the Elexon feed above does not capture, since it only meters transmission-connected generation.
  • NESO Carbon Intensity API — 30-minute national and regional carbon intensity, a 48-hour forecast, and fuel-mix percentages used as a fallback solar estimate for history older than the embedded generation dataset's rolling ~2 month window.

Neither Elexon nor NESO have reviewed, endorsed, or approved this dashboard or any figure derived from their data.

Methodology

This dashboard performs independent processing on top of the raw sources listed above. In summary:

  • Settlement alignment. 5-minute FUELINST readings are floored to their enclosing half-hour settlement period to align with the half-hourly NESO feeds.
  • Embedded generation. Solar and wind figures combine Elexon's transmission-connected metered output with NESO's embedded generation estimate, so totals reflect the whole of GB, not just transmission-connected sites.
  • Total demand. Calculated as active generation + net interconnector transfers + storage discharge − storage charging, so that per-fuel percentages sum to approximately 100% of demand.
  • Decarbonisation share. The zero-carbon percentage sums wind, solar, nuclear and hydro output as a share of total generation.
  • Refresh cadence. Data is fetched and republished roughly every 15 minutes by an automated pipeline; historical views (today, previous day, week, month, year) are regenerated from a local data warehouse on every run.

Full implementation detail, including the data pipeline's source code, is published in the GitHub repository, for anyone who wants to audit or reproduce the methodology exactly.
